MAPPINGS
Watch film →

Through self-regulating systems in which performed sound, electronics and score are interconnected in real time, MAPPINGS takes three field recordings, one made in London and two in Mexico City, as raw materials which are mapped onto the ensemble over the course of the piece. Performed by Plus-Minus Ensemble, recorded at The Empire Studio and filmed by Anna Francesca Jennings.
